History of Manchester City

From St. Mark's (West Gorton) to the modern Etihad era, this timeline keeps the club's defining moments in a clear, readable Bootstrap layout.

The Founding

1880: St. Mark's (West Gorton)

The club was founded by Anna Connell and the wardens of St. Mark's Church as a response to the social problems of industrial Manchester.

By 1894 the club had become Manchester City FC, a name that represented the whole city and set the foundation for everything that followed.
